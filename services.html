Small Business Consulting

Running a small business is a lot. The day-to-day operations, customer relationships, and everything in between add up fast, and the tech side of things can feel like a second job. I help small business owners get set up with the right tools, automate the tasks that eat up their time, and make technology work for them instead of against them.

I've worked inside tech companies (Sourcegraph, Atlassian), built software, and spent years managing restaurant operations including POS systems across multiple locations. I understand both sides of the counter.

What I can help with

POS & tech setup

Point-of-sale systems, payment processing, inventory tools, and the software that runs your day-to-day.

AI solutions

Workflow automation

Connecting your tools so they work together, reducing manual data entry and freeing up your time for what matters.
